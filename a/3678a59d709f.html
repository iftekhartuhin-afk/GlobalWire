Pope Leo Becomes First Pontiff to Vote in US General Election

Al Jazeera English · 2026-10-31

The Vatican confirmed that Pope Leo has taken part in the US general election, marking the first time a pontiff has done so. He cast a mail-in ballot in Chicago, his native city, ahead of the November 3 midterms.
