Russia and Ukraine trade blame for cyberattacks as Kherson truce ends

France 24 · 2026-11-02

Moscow and Kyiv blamed each other for cyberattacks on Monday, following the expiration of a fragile ceasefire in the Kherson region. Russia reported a hack affecting its emergency alerts app shortly after Kyiv announced a compromise of its air raid warning system. The developments have raised concerns about renewed hostilities.
