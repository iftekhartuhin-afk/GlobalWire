Consumers pay 300-440 taka extra for cooking gas despite government action

According to Prothom Alo, consumers are paying an additional 300 to 440 taka for cooking gas. The government’s attempts to stop sales at inflated prices have not succeeded. The higher price range is reported across the country. The issue has drawn attention to the effectiveness of regulatory actions.

The Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission (BERC) has raised commissions for gas traders, yet the regulated price ceiling is still being ignored. The set price for cooking gas remains unchanged, but sellers continue to charge above that level.
