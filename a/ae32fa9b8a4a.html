Democrat Scott Colom Wins Mississippi Senate Seat, First Black Senator Since Reconstruction

According to France 24, Democrat Scott Colom secured a narrow victory over incumbent Republican Senator Cindy Hyde‑Smith after the final count was completed on Wednesday. The result flips the Mississippi Senate seat to the Democratic Party, contributing to the broader pattern of Democratic gains in the current midterm elections as the party of former President Donald Trump experiences a series of defeats.

DW reported that Colom’s win marks the first time in more than 140 years that Mississippi will be represented by a Black senator, a milestone dating back to the Reconstruction era. The outcome is described as a historic upset and underscores the shifting political dynamics in the traditionally conservative Southern state.
