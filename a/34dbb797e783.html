First 'Alor Janmadin' in Sunamganj features Hasan Raja, Shah Abdul Karim songs

Sunamganj hosted its inaugural 'Alor Janmadin' celebration, featuring songs by legendary Bengali composers Hasan Raja and Shah Abdul Karim. The event was reported by Prothom Alo.

Renowned musicians Sohel Rana and Rita Achary performed the selected repertoire, while Nurfiza Haq and Debolina Talukdar presented dance numbers. Poetry was recited by Safwan Hasan, Jubin Talukdar and a group of children from the Bati Ghar cultural centre. The program combined music, dance and poetry to honor the composers' legacy.
