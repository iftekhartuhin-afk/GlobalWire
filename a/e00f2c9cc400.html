UN report says climate adaptation aid to developing nations is far below needs

A United Nations report highlighted that the amount of climate‑adaptation funding reaching developing nations falls far short of the levels required to address climate impacts. The assessment points to a significant shortfall in financial support from wealthier countries, which hampers the ability of vulnerable states to implement necessary adaptation measures.

The report, cited by Al Jazeera English, underscores the urgency of scaling up contributions to meet the growing needs of developing regions facing climate‑related challenges. It calls for increased commitment from affluent nations to bridge the funding gap and support effective adaptation strategies.
