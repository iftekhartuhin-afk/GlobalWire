Trump to join Xi and Putin in Shenzhen for historic trilateral summit

President Donald Trump is traveling to Shenzhen for a series of Asian meetings that will include a rare trilateral summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin, NPR World reports.

France 24 notes that Xi and Putin met on Wednesday ahead of Trump’s arrival. Trump has said Ukraine will be on the agenda, while Xi views the war as not China’s problem, prompting concerns that Kyiv’s fate could be discussed.

The summit is slated to address trade and security matters, underscoring the significance of a three‑way dialogue among the United States, China and Russia.
