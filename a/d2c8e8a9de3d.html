Families in northern Yemen struggle to afford food despite market availability

According to Al Jazeera English, food items such as flour and cooking oil continue to be delivered to markets in the northern Yemeni cities of Hodeidah and Sanaa. Nevertheless, the products are priced beyond the reach of many households.

The severely unaffordable costs, combined with a lack of work and insufficient aid, mean that families remain hungry even though food is physically available in the shops. This situation underscores the broader economic and humanitarian crisis affecting the north of Yemen.
