Three teenagers catch shrimp, crabs and fish in Sundarbans river, women also fish there

Prothom Alo reports that three teenagers were seen catching shrimp, crabs and fish in a river within the Sundarbans mangrove region.

Consequently, participants across different ages and genders engage in the river’s harvest.

Prothom Alo notes the regular presence of women on these fishing outings.
