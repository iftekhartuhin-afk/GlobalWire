Rash Purnima Marks Celebration of Ras Lila, a Dance Festival

Ras Lila is observed on the full moon of the Rash month, as reported by Prothom Alo. The term “Lila” translates to dance, and the festival centers on this performance. The celebration is described as the original festival of pure love.

On this day, the Gopis, devotees of Krishna, wait for the deity’s summons. The supreme companion of Krishna is described as eager to receive the call of his divine play. The anticipation reflects the devotional bond between the Gopis and Krishna.
